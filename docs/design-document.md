## 1. Problem Statement
In Uganda, small-scale farmers face significant challenges in accessing fair markets for their produce. They often rely on middlemen who take large cuts of the profit, and they lack a centralized platform to connect directly with bulk buyers. Furthermore, there is a significant gap in accessible agricultural extension services and nutrition education. 

To solve this, we are proposing AgriLink, a Flutter-based mobile application. The app will allow farmers to list produce, buyers to place orders, and users to access vital agronomic and nutritional advisory content.
